Company Profile

Precision engineered for high-stress industrial sectors

DMVEX GLOBAL is focused on petrochemical-grade bolting, structural bolts and nuts, and precision-machined components. We serve industrial applications where product specification, dimensional conformity, material identification and inspection records are important to the supply process.

Our product offering includes stud bolts, threaded studs, nuts and other bolting components manufactured or supplied to the applicable customer and product requirements. Material grade, coating, testing scope and inspection documentation should be agreed against the customer's technical specification and service conditions.
